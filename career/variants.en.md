# Targeted CV variants

Each variant changes **emphasis, not facts**. The canonical experience timeline and evidence live in [profile.en.md](profile.en.md). Use only the most relevant skills and experience bullets for the opening half-page of a two-page application.

## Variant A — Software Architect / Principal Engineer

**Headline:** Software Architect | Principal-Level Backend Engineering | Modernization & Integrations

### Professional summary

Software architect and hands-on backend engineer with 16+ years of experience designing, modernizing, and evolving business-critical systems. I specialize in turning complex requirements and legacy constraints into explicit domain boundaries, maintainable architectures, and reliable integrations. My experience spans PHP/Symfony/Laravel, relational data design, SQL performance, and technical leadership. I combine pragmatic architectural judgment with AI-assisted development practices to accelerate delivery without compromising review, testing, or ownership.

### Lead with

- Nord Partner: architectural refactoring and integration design.
- KISS digital: backend architecture across commercial projects.
- Bikeparks.pl: modular monolith, domain rules, SQL read models, performance, end-to-end technical ownership.

**Avoid:** implying formal Principal Engineer employment if that was not the job title. This is an application positioning variant.

## Variant B — Technical Lead / Engineering Manager

**Headline:** Technical Lead | Engineering Leadership | Architecture & Delivery

### Professional summary

Technical leader and software architect with 16+ years of engineering experience and a background in leading developers across software-house and in-house environments. I combine hands-on backend expertise with mentoring, architecture ownership, and the ability to turn business goals into achievable technical plans. I focus on engineering clarity, code quality, team effectiveness, and delivery under real operational constraints. My recent work also includes introducing structured AI-assisted development workflows with explicit QA and human decision gates.

### Lead with

- Fashion Trends Group: day-to-day team leadership, mentoring, collaboration with business.
- KISS digital: technical leadership across projects and developer mentoring.
- Nord Partner: coordination of architecture and stakeholder requirements.
- Bikeparks.pl: engineering workflow, task decomposition, quality gates and delivery ownership.

**Needs evidence before applying to senior management roles:** direct-report count, hiring/performance responsibility, budget ownership, and management tenure. Do not claim these until confirmed.

## Variant C — Senior PHP / Symfony Backend Engineer

**Headline:** Senior Backend Engineer | PHP · Symfony · Laravel · PostgreSQL

### Professional summary

Senior PHP backend engineer with 16+ years of commercial experience delivering and modernizing web applications and integrated business systems. I work hands-on with Symfony, Laravel, PostgreSQL, Docker, APIs, and legacy code, with a strong focus on correctness, maintainability, and SQL performance. Beyond implementation, I contribute architectural decisions, code reviews, automated testing, and production-minded troubleshooting. I use AI coding tools as part of a reviewed engineering workflow, while retaining responsibility for the delivered code.

### Lead with

- Concrete PHP/Symfony/Laravel experience and recent backend architecture.
- Bikeparks.pl: Symfony, Doctrine, PostgreSQL, QA and deployment work.
- Nord Partner and Quad: refactoring, integrations, reliability.
- Targeted examples of SQL performance and production debugging.

**Avoid:** over-indexing on “AI transformation” for a hands-on backend vacancy. Demonstrate current coding depth first.

## Adaptation checklist

1. Match the headline to the vacancy title without inventing a past role.
2. Use one relevant summary above; trim to 3–4 sentences if space is limited.
3. Choose 5–8 relevant core skills and 2–3 quantified achievements, if independently verified.
4. Keep Bikeparks.pl as a flagship project, but choose the most relevant 2–3 bullets.
5. Maintain the same factual employment dates, employers, and project states in every variant.
6. English remains B2 (self-assessed). Avoid claiming C1, fluent, or native proficiency.
7. Export each application to an ATS-friendly, mostly single-column PDF only after fact-checking.
